01 — Product Guide

Product Types

Snoeks conversion product range explained

Crew Cab
The Crew Cab is a perfect solution for combining the transport of passengers and cargo. Transport safely, efficiently, and comfortably in one drive. The ideal solution for entrepreneurs to move crew and gear on the job and enjoy family outings during free time. We develop in close collaboration with our partners to ensure the product fits seamlessly with the vehicle and meets the needs of the customer and the end-user. As a preferred partner for worldwide LCV manufacturers we've built a large portfolio of Crew Cab solutions.
Flex Cab
The FlexCab solution offers the best of both worlds. Offering more flexibility in the usability of the vehicle. The comfortable three-seater bench and partition wall can be folded as a whole in a forward position, creating a substantially larger cargo area that is easy accessible from the sliding door.
Partition Wall
By adding a partition wall in a closed panel van, both the driver and passengers will be protected from shifting cargo. Additionally, our partition walls prevent any unpleasant odors or dust coming from the cargo. It also improves the isolation in the driver's compartment.
