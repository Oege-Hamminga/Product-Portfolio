02 — Product Guide

Product Types

Snoeks conversion product range explained

Crew Cab v1 OEM
Factory-integrated crew cab supplied via the vehicle manufacturer's order system. Full type approval (CoC) from the OEM. Available on Stellantis, Toyota and Renault platforms. Ordered as a factory option at point of vehicle build.
Crew Cab v2 After-fit
Snoeks-engineered conversion applied to existing panel vans after factory delivery. Maximum flexibility for retrofit and stock orders across all supported brands. Includes Certificate of Conformity (CoC) per active country. Configurable for wheelbase, glass type, upholstery and trim level.
Flex Cab
Modular dual-use system converting between passenger cabin and cargo configuration. Uses a quick-release floor rail system with fold-flat seat assemblies. Ideal for mixed-use operators requiring versatility without a permanent crew cab commitment.
Partition Wall
Steel partition separating driver compartment from cargo area. Standard and extended widths, with solid steel, polycarbonate, tempered glass or steel mesh infill options. Acoustic insulation and vinyl lining available. Suits OEM combi vans and after-fit applications.
